## Opportunities

**We welcome inquiries from prospective Ph.D. and master's students, as well as motivated undergraduates, research assistants, and visiting students.**

## Research Training and Collaboration

We welcome students interested in control theory, artificial intelligence, embodied intelligence, and multi-robot systems. Our approach to training emphasizes sound theoretical foundations, provable methods, and working systems. Research combines theoretical analysis, algorithm design, and robot experiments, with a focus on the closed loop from intelligent decisions to embodied execution.

Through the National Engineering Research Center for Robot Visual Perception and Control Technology and our research collaborators, students may have opportunities for joint research and academic exchange with Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan University, Tsinghua University, Southeast University, and the University of Groningen. They may also work on engineering problems connected to platforms such as Tencent Robotics X, State Grid, and China Southern Power Grid.
