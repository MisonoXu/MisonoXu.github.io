## About

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at Hunan University. I am also a core member of the National Engineering Research Center for Robot Visual Perception and Control Technology, working with the team of Academician Yaonan Wang and Dean Hui Zhang.

My research focuses on distributed optimization, game-theoretic decision-making, and cooperative control for complex networked systems and collective intelligence. Drawing on nonlinear control, stability theory, and networked control, I study how agents use local information to make collective optimization and game-theoretic decisions, and how networked feedback turns these decisions into coordinated behavior subject to physical dynamics and execution constraints. Applications include unmanned swarms and multi-robot systems.

I have authored or coauthored more than 30 published or accepted journal and conference papers, including 10 first-author papers in control journals such as IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, Automatica, and other IEEE Transactions journals. One paper was recognized as an ESI Highly Cited Paper in 2022. I am the principal investigator of a project supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China under its Basic Research Program for Young Students (Doctoral Students).
